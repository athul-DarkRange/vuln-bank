Privacy policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This policy explains what personal data ISB collects, why we collect it and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

We collect the information you give us when you open and use an account, such as your name, username, contact details, transaction history and the documents you upload. We also collect technical information such as device type and log data to keep the service running.

2. How we use it

We use your information to provide and secure your account, process payments, meet legal obligations, answer your questions and improve our services. We do not use your information for purposes that are incompatible with these.

3. How we protect it

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls, monitoring and regular reviews of our systems.

4. Who we share it with

We share data with service providers who help us run ISB, with payment networks to complete transactions, and with authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, restrict or object to its use, or request deletion where the law allows. Contact us to make a request.

6. Children's privacy

ISB accounts are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and tell you about significant changes.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@isb.example.
